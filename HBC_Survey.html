About this resource

This curated survey collects publications across research groups. The IC timeline begins with the earliest IC paper currently included, in 2006. Earlier foundational references remain in the bibliography. Counts are publications, not unique chips. The search was refreshed on 13 November 2026; coverage may be incomplete.

Acknowledgements. The author thanks Gourab Barik, Baibhab Chatterjee, Shovan Maity, Mayukh Nath, Debayan Das, Donghyun Seo, David Yang, and Arunashish Datta for their contributions to V1 and V2 of the survey.

AI usage. Artificial intelligence (AI), including large language models accessed through OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex, has been used for literature discovery, bibliographic organization, and preparation of the website, manuscript, and scripts. AI-assisted content may contain mistakes. If you find a mistake, please let us know.

Conflict of interest. Shreyas Sen is a founder, Chairman and Chief Technology Officer of Quasistatics, Inc. (doing business as Ixana). This affiliation is disclosed as a potential competing interest. The catalogue includes publications coauthored by Shreyas Sen.

Inclusion requests. We may have missed relevant papers. If you would like a relevant paper published in a Tier-1 journal or conference to be considered for inclusion, please email shreyas@purdue.edu with its title, full citation, and DOI or source link. Requests will be reviewed for inclusion.

Corrections. If you find a mistake, please email shreyas@purdue.edu with the paper identifier or DOI and a description of the error. Reported issues will be reviewed, and confirmed corrections will be incorporated into subsequent updates. shreyas@purdue.edu
